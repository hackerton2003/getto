
GHETTO PLATFORM
Country-Wide Customer Accommodation Platform
Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Functional Requirements, Non-Functional Requirements and Database Design — Development-Ready Edition

1. Introduction
This document specifies the complete functional requirements, non-functional requirements and database design for the Ghetto Platform — a country-wide system connecting university customers with accommodation owners for the discovery, booking and payment of houses and rooms, supported by a Web-GIS map component.
Requirements are organized by system module (Sections 2–3). The database design (Section 5) has been updated to store room ratings, customer favourites, and room dimensions, and reflects every entity referenced across all modules below.

4.9 Design Notes
•	Distance from campus is calculated at query time from stored latitude/longitude coordinates (Haversine formula); no separate distance table is stored, avoiding stale or oversized pre-computed data.
•	Room photos are limited to 5 and video to 1 per room; this limit is enforced at the application level, not the database schema.
•	Ratings, favourites and room dimensions — previously missing — are now covered by the reviews, favourites and rooms tables respectively.
•	Geography is normalized as regions → districts → wards → streets so new areas can be added without redesigning the schema, per the country-wide scalability requirement.
•	audit_logs consolidates the security-audit trail (Module 18) and system-monitoring log (Module 20) into a single table to avoid duplicate logging structures.





GHETTO PLATFORM FEATURES

1. Customer Registration
Customers can create personal accounts using their basic information such as name, phone number,
email, university, and customer ID. The account allows the platform to identify and manage each
customer's activities securely. Customers can later update their profile information when necessary.
Phone or email verification can be used to improve account security. This feature provides a
personalized starting point for finding and booking accommodation.

2. University Selection
Customers select the university they are attending or planning to attend. The system then uses the
selected university as the main reference point for accommodation searches. This allows customers
to see available rooms specifically around their campus. Universities can be added or managed
by administrators. The feature makes searching faster and more relevant.

3. Accommodation Search
Customers can search for accommodation based on price, room type, distance, facilities, and
availability. Search filters allow customers to reduce hundreds of properties to those that match
their needs. Results can be sorted by distance, price, rating, or availability. The system displays
important information before the customer contacts or books a property. This reduces the time and
effort required to find suitable accommodation.

4. Map-Based Accommodation Discovery
The platform displays accommodation locations on an interactive digital map. Customers can
visually identify properties near their university and understand their geographical position. Each
map marker can display basic information such as room type, price, and availability. Customers
can select a marker to open the full accommodation details. This feature helps customers
understand where they would actually be living before making a decision.

5. Accommodation Details
Each accommodation has a dedicated page containing its price, location, room type, facilities,
availability, and description. Customers can review the information before deciding whether to
book. The page can also display the distance from the selected university. Important information
is organized in one place instead of requiring customers to contact several brokers. This improves
transparency during the accommodation-search process.

6. Photos and Short Videos
Accommodation owners can upload photos showing rooms, bathrooms, kitchens, compounds,
and other important areas. They can also upload short videos to provide a more realistic view of
the property. Customers can inspect the accommodation remotely before deciding to visit it. Media
uploads should be reviewed according to the platform's verification rules. This feature helps
reduce misleading descriptions and unnecessary physical visits.

7. Distance Calculation
The system automatically calculates the distance between an accommodation and the customer's
university. It can display information such as "850 meters from campus" or an estimated walking
distance. Customers can use this information when comparing different properties. The calculation
can be supported by geographical coordinates stored for universities and properties. This makes
location one of the measurable factors in accommodation selection.

8. Owner Dashboard
Accommodation owners receive a dedicated dashboard for managing their properties after their accounts have been created and activated by authorized Super Admins or staff. They can
add properties, update room availability, upload media, and manage booking requests. The
dashboard can also show information about active rooms, bookings, and payments. Owners
cannot perform administrative actions reserved for the platform administrators. This provides
owners with a centralized way to manage their accommodation business.
9. Add Accommodation
Owners can submit information about their accommodation through a structured form. They
provide details such as property name, location, room type, price, facilities, security information,
and available rooms. The system allows them to select the property's location using a map.
Photos and videos can also be attached to the property. After submission, the property enters the
administrative verification process before becoming publicly available.

10. Accommodation Verification
Every submitted accommodation can pass through an administrative verification process before
appearing publicly. Administrators can review the owner's information, property details,
location, images, and other required evidence. Properties can be marked as pending, approved,
rejected, or suspended. Approved properties can display a verification indicator to customers. This
creates a structured trust mechanism between customers, owners, and the platform.

11. Booking System
Customers can request a room directly through the platform instead of relying entirely on brokers.
The system records the selected accommodation, room, customer, price, and booking status. The
owner receives the request and confirms whether the requested room is actually available. After
confirmation, the customer proceeds to the payment stage. This creates a clear digital record of the
accommodation booking process.

12. Payment System
After an owner confirms the accommodation, the customer can pay the required rent and service
fee through the platform. The system calculates the total amount automatically, including the
defined 10% service fee. Every transaction receives a unique reference and payment status.
Payment information is stored securely and linked to the corresponding booking. The
architecture should allow different Tanzanian payment providers to be integrated without
changing the entire booking system.

13. Notifications
The platform sends notifications when important actions occur. Customers can receive
notifications about booking confirmations, payment results, cancellations, and other important
updates. Owners can receive notifications about new booking requests and successful payments.
Notifications can eventually be delivered through in-app alerts, email, SMS, or other supported
channels. This keeps both parties informed without requiring them to constantly check the
platform.

14. Reviews and Ratings
Customers who have completed verified bookings can review their accommodation experience.
They can provide feedback about factors such as cleanliness, security, location, owner behavior,
and value. Reviews are connected to actual bookings to reduce fake feedback. Owners can use
genuine feedback to identify areas that need improvement. Customers can use previous
experiences as additional information when comparing accommodations.

15. Favorites
Customers can save accommodations they are interested in for later consideration. Saved
properties appear in a personal favorites section. Customers can return to these properties without
repeating their original search. This is useful when a customer wants to compare several
accommodation options. The feature makes the decision-making process more convenient.

16. Accommodation Comparison
Customers can select multiple properties and compare their important characteristics in one view.
The comparison can include price, distance, room type, facilities, security features, availability,
and ratings. This prevents customers from repeatedly switching between different accommodation
pages. The information is presented side by side for easier understanding. The final
accommodation choice remains with the customer.

17. Complaints and Disputes
Customers can report problems such as incorrect property information, unavailable rooms,
payment issues, or other accommodation-related concerns. Each complaint receives a record that
administrators can investigate. Administrators can communicate with the relevant customer and
owner during the investigation. The system can track the complaint from submission to
resolution. This creates an accountability mechanism for platform users.

18. Admin Dashboard
Administrators manage the major operations of the Ghetto platform from one centralized
dashboard. They can manage users, universities, properties, rooms, bookings, payments, reviews,
complaints, and verification requests. The dashboard can display statistics about platform
activity. Administrative permissions should be controlled according to user roles. This allows the
platform to operate under centralized governance while maintaining proper access control.
